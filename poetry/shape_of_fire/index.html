Shape Of Fire

Sanya Mansoor · Sep 20, 2026 · Poetry

The shape of ﬁre, where
a ﬂicker abandons the ember.

Dirt at the border, the last person
killed in a war.

The way the ﬁrst letter of your
name sounds when someone speaks it.

The curve where the brown
of your eyes turns to black.

When a child glances
upon the vulnerability of their parents.

The year that they forgive them.

The moment when a moth realizes death is imminent.

The bottom plank of your coffin nestling into the ground.

The ﬁnal drop of water from ablution trickling down your body.

The crevice of a cracked mirror, jagged edges turning soft,
finally meeting my own gaze.
